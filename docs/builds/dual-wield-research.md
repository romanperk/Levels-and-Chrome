# Dual-Wield Build Research

Working notes for `src/content/guides/dual-wield-gunslinger.yaml`. Collected on 2026-11-07. Most of this comes from third-party guides written soon after patch 2.5, and the owner corrects it while playing.

## Target at Level 60

| Attribute | Target | Guides |
|---|---|---|
| Cool | 20 | All |
| Technical | 20 | All |
| Reflexes | 20 | games.gg, KeenGamer. Others say 18 |
| Body | 18 | games.gg, KeenGamer. Others say 20 |
| Intelligence | 3 | All |

Reflexes 20 and Body 18 fit the Sandevistan focus, but the two sources that recommend it are near-identical and may not be independent. The total is exactly 81 points.

## Attribute Route

The guides agree on this order: Cool 9, Cool 20, Technical 20, Reflexes, then Body.

| Levels | Spend on | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Character creation | Cool 6, Reflexes 6, Technical 4, Body 3, Intelligence 3 |
| 2-15 | Cool | Cool 9 at level 4, 15 at level 10, 20 at level 15 |
| 16-21 | Reflexes **(proposed change)** | Reflexes 12, for better Sandevistans |
| 22-37 | Technical | Technical 20 |
| 38-45 | Reflexes | Reflexes 20 |
| 46-60 | Body | Body 18 |

**Still to decide:** following the guides strictly leaves Reflexes at 6 until level 32. One source lists Zetatech Mk.2 needing Reflexes 9 and Mk.3 needing 12, so levels 16-21 go to Reflexes here. Body stays at 3 until level 46, which may feel too fragile, so playtesting may move some points earlier.

## Perks

**Double Trouble branch:** Six points, all at Cool 9. Ranks 1-2 give dual-wielding and Heywood Hurricane. The others are Two Right Hands, Triple Tequila, Keep 'Em Comin' and Splash of Love. The guides disagree on the order; the draft takes Two Right Hands first for hip-fire stamina, then Triple Tequila, which the other perks build on.

**Cool:** Focus (Cool 9), Deadeye (Cool 15), Run 'N' Gun (Cool 20). High Noon, Long Shot and California Reaper have no listed requirement.

**Reflexes:** Dash, Air Dash, Steady Grip, Can't Touch This, Muscle Memory.

**Technical:** All Things Cyber (two ranks, cheaper integumentary and skeletal cyberware), Renaissance Punk (Technical 9, needs All Things Cyber, +4 capacity per attribute at 9 or higher), License to Chrome, Edgerunner (Technical 20, up to +50 capacity at -0.5% max health per point over), Ticking Time Bomb.

**Body:** Adrenaline Rush, and either Painkiller or Pain to Gain, as sources name it differently.

**Relic:** Vulnerability Analytics, Machine Learning.

Perk points can be refunded freely. The attribute reset is once per character.

## Cyberware

Capacity costs, exact tiers and vendors weren't in any source we could read, so these aren't in the guide yet. Guides also disagree on some slot names. All ripperdocs reportedly sell almost everything, so availability depends on your level.

| Slot | Recommended |
|---|---|
| Operating system | Dynalar or Zetatech from level 10, Falcon from level 30, Apogee from level 40 |
| Hands | Ballistic Coprocessor, Shock Absorber |
| Face | Kiroshi Cockatrice Optics |
| Nervous | Kerenzikov |
| Circulatory | Blood Pump, Biomonitor |
| Integumentary | Peripheral Inverse (Phantom Liberty iconic), or Optical Camo |
| Legs | Reinforced Tendons |
| Frontal cortex | Axolotl (for finishing all of Regina's gigs) |

**Sandevistan pick:** guides agree on Apogee or Falcon late, but disagree on Zetatech's numbers. Zetatech Mk.1 is listed at 6,000 to 7,500 eddies, with a time slow of either 30% or 62%. Check it in game.

## Guns

Only Power Pistols and Power Revolvers can be dual-wielded. Different guns can be paired.

| Gun | Type | Where |
|---|---|---|
| La Chingona Dorada (two) | Power pistol | Jackie's ofrenda at El Coyote Cojo, after Heroes |
| Kongou | Power pistol | Yorinobu's penthouse, The Heist |
| Dying Night | Power pistol | Wilson, The Gun side job |
| Malorian Arms 3516 | Power pistol | Grayson, Chippin' In. Guides suggest it as a late-game partner for a Dorada |
| Archangel | Power revolver | Kerry, A Like Supreme. Revolver option with an Overture |

One guide says iconic effects stay active when dual-wielding. Another found that the 3516's melee action is replaced by Heywood Hurricane.
